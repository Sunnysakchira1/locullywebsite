> before you start

Questions we get every week.

Does this work outside healthcare?

Yes. The mechanism is the same whether buyers are choosing a clinic, a restaurant, a hotel or a condo — they ask a model, and it names two or three. Our deepest results are in Bangkok healthcare, and we'll show you those numbers rather than invent yours.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets your page ranked. AIO gets your name into the answer that sits above the page. They overlap — good SEO helps — but ranking #1 and being named are different outcomes, measured differently. We report citations, not positions.

How long until I show up in ChatGPT?

Perplexity and AI Overviews can pick up new sources within days. Sustained presence in ChatGPT and Gemini typically builds over three to six months, depending on each model's index schedule. We don't promise placement in any specific model.

What's actually in the free audit?

We run a real prompt from your category against a live model, and show you the answer it gives: who's named, who's cited, and whether you appear. Plus a technical read of your site — AI crawler access, schema, llms.txt. No call required to get it.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and neither can anyone else — the models decide. What we guarantee is measurement: you'll see exactly which prompts you were absent from at the start, and exactly which ones changed. If nothing moves, you'll know within 60 days.

Do I need to stop my current SEO?

No. If you have an agency doing technical SEO and content, that work still helps. We handle the layer above it. Plenty of clients run both.
