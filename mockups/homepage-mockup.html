How is AI Visibility Optimization different from SEO? −

Traditional SEO gets you ranking in Google's blue links — AIO gets you cited as the answer in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude. Both matter, but AI citations drive 4.4× higher conversion because users arrive pre-sold. We do both, with AIO bundled into every SEO retainer at no extra cost.

How quickly will I see results? +

Most clients see their first AI citations within 60–90 days. The Physio Clinic, Bangkok case study went from 3 to 13 AI bookings/month in just the first 30 days of active work. Full compounding effect typically kicks in at month 3–4.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for? +

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, Claude (Anthropic), Bing Copilot, and Gemini. We track your brand's citation rate across all major platforms and report monthly on share of voice, citation frequency, and ranking position.

I'm already doing SEO. Do I need this? +

Traditional SEO and AIO use different signals. A site that ranks well on Google can be completely invisible to AI — and vice versa. Your free audit will show you exactly where you stand on both dimensions. Most clients discover they're strong on Google but scoring near zero in AI visibility.

What does it cost? +

Retainers start at $1,500/month. AIO is bundled with SEO — not sold separately. We also offer one-off packages for audits and infrastructure setup. The free audit gives you a full picture of your current AI visibility with no obligation.

Do you work with businesses outside healthcare? +

Yes. Our methodology works across verticals — healthcare, restaurants, hospitality, property, professional services. If your customers use AI to research purchases, you need AI visibility. The clinic case studies are our most measurable proof, but the approach is industry-agnostic.
