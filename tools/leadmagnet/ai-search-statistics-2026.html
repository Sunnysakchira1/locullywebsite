03 · Zero-Click & Behaviour
03 — Zero-Click & Behaviour Shift
The decline of the click
The bigger story behind AI search is the decline of the click itself. Users get the answer and never leave the results page.
25%
Forecast drop in traditional search volume by 2026 as users shift to AI.
Gartner · Feb 2024
68%
Of US Google searches end without a click — up from 60% in 2024.
SparkToro / Similarweb · Jun 2026
15–25%
Estimated organic traffic loss as ~80% rely on zero-click results.
Bain & Company · Feb 2025
41%
Of Gen Z turn to social platforms first when searching, ahead of search engines.
Sprout Social · May 2025
Why it matters
  • The click that traditional SEO was built to win is no longer guaranteed — most searches now end on the page.
  • Organic traffic is structurally shrinking, so ranking visibility no longer equals website traffic.
  • Younger audiences are diversifying across social and AI — relying on one search channel is now a real risk.
